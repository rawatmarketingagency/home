Our process

Four simple steps, so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

1

Understand

We start by learning your business.

  • Talk through your business, products and customers
  • Set clear goals: calls, enquiries or sales
  • Agree on budget and what success looks like
2

Plan

A simple plan you can follow.

  • Choose the right channels for your goal and budget
  • Set a timeline and monthly targets
  • Share clear scope and costs before we start
3

Execute

Your campaigns and work go live.

  • Set up SEO, ads, content or your website
  • Launch, test and fix early
  • Track every lead and every rupee spent
4

Report & Improve

Regular updates and steady improvement.

  • Easy-to-read reports on traffic, leads and performance
  • Keep what works and stop what does not
  • Ongoing optimisation for better results
